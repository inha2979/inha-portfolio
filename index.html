INFOBYTE · WHAT I CHANGED ALONG THE WAY

How I improved the videos

01 · Static → Dynamic

Early videos relied on static visuals, so viewers had little reason to keep watching. I introduced camera movement — zooms, pans and motion between scenes — to hold attention from shot to shot.

02 · Product announcement → Why it matters to you

The first lines originally just named the new tool. I rewrote them to lead with what the technology could mean for viewers, so the video feels relevant before it starts explaining.

Before: “AI Astra, newly launched”After: “The AI that replaces humans has arrived!”
